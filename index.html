Step 2: Implemented Security Controls

Toggle ON for controls currently implemented and operational within your organization.

1. Hardware/Software Firewall PROTECT
Monitors and filters incoming/outgoing network traffic with default-deny inbound rules.
2. Antivirus / Endpoint Protection (EDR) PROTECT
Centrally managed next-gen endpoint protection with behavioral anti-ransomware capabilities.
3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) PROTECT
Enforced two-step verification for corporate email, SaaS suites, and VPN logins.
4. Regular & Immutable Backups RECOVER
Follows 3-2-1 backup strategy with verified offline or cloud-immutable storage copies.
5. Data Encryption (At Rest & Transit) PROTECT
AES-256 full disk encryption on laptops (BitLocker/FileVault) and TLS 1.3 across networks.
6. Employee Cybersecurity Training PROTECT
Ongoing employee awareness sessions with simulated phishing drills and credential hygiene.
7. Regular Software & Patch Updates PROTECT
Scheduled patching for operating systems, browsers, and critical third-party tools within 14 days.
8. Access Control & RBAC PROTECT
Principle of Least Privilege enforced; standard staff stripped of administrative privileges.
9. Security Event Monitoring & Logging DETECT
Centralized retention and alerting for failed authentications and suspicious privilege elevation.
10. Incident Response Plan RESPOND
Formally documented response procedures, legal contacts, and recovery checklists for cyber incidents.
